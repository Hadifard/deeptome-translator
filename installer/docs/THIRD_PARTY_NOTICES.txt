DEEPTOME TRANSLATOR - THIRD-PARTY COMPONENTS
============================================

DeepTome Translator uses the work of other authors.  None of it is inside the installer: Setup downloads each
component from its own publisher onto your computer, where it stays under its own licence.  The licence text of
every Python package is installed with it in
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\DeepTome\python\Lib\site-packages\<package>-<version>.dist-info

The licences below are stated as the projects name them; the project pages are authoritative.
